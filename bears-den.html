About the property

Camp right on the Appalachian Trail

Bears Den sits on 66 acres of Blue Ridge forest, five miles from the Shenandoah River and 15 miles from Purcellville. It's remote enough to feel wild, but easy to reach by car.

The land was once the summer home of an opera singer. Today the 1930s stone house, built from local hand-hammered stone, is where your host lives and runs a hostel for trail hikers.

  • Sunset overlook a 5–10 minute walk from camp
  • Bear Chase Brewery half a mile across the road
  • Named 2nd best Hipcamp in Virginia (2023)
